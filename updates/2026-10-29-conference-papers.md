## Source verification

- **EMNLP:** The [ACL Anthology main-conference record](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.1343/) matches KairosAgent's title and authors. The [author project page](https://mldi.group/KairosAgent/) provides no implementation, so code is listed as None.
- **ACM MM:** The [official technical programme](https://2026.acmmm.org/site/technical-programme.html) lists F-LLM (Poster-Wa-H5) and MarsTSC (Poster-We-J23) in the Main Track. Authors match the arXiv papers and link to the listed repositories. F-LLM's conference title omits “Provably Stable”; the institutional announcement links the arXiv version listed here.
- **CIKM:** The [official programme](https://cikm2026.diag.uniroma1.it/program/) identifies Time-R1 and UPLOTS as full research papers and directly links their repositories. The arXiv titles and authors match.
- **ICDM:** The [official sessions](https://icdm2026.neu.edu.cn/DetailedSessions/list.htm) list FADTI (DM408) and ScenarioDiff (DM2071) as Research Track Short papers in the main conference. FADTI's arXiv paper and repository reciprocally match. ScenarioDiff's arXiv paper explicitly identifies itself as an extended version of the accepted paper; the author-linked anonymous repository contains model and training files and links back to that arXiv paper. Its hosting currently states availability until December 6, 2026.
